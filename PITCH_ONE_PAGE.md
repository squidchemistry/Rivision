# 📱 RIVISION - ONE-PAGE PITCH

---

## 🎯 THE CONCEPT

**Rivision** is an AI-powered learning platform that generates personalized revision questions from any study material in seconds. Students upload PDFs, we deliver adaptive questions. They study smarter, pass harder exams.

---

## 🔥 THE PROBLEM

- 70% of student study time is wasted on ineffective revision
- Traditional study methods are boring and one-size-fits-all
- Quality tutoring is expensive ($30-100/hour)
- Students need scientific, adaptive learning tools

**Market Size:** $2.5B (revision-focused EdTech segment)

---

## 💡 THE SOLUTION

### How Rivision Works

```
1. Student uploads study material (PDF, notes, textbook)
   ↓
2. Our AI generates 50-100 revision questions in 5 seconds
   ↓
3. Student attempts questions, gets instant feedback
   ↓
4. Algorithm adapts difficulty based on performance
   ↓
5. Spaced repetition ensures long-term retention
   ↓
6. Dashboard shows progress, streaks, weak areas
```

### Key Differentiator
- **Groq API** - Fastest AI inference (sub-1 second)
- **Spaced Repetition** - Scientifically proven
- **Adaptive Learning** - Difficulty adjusts in real-time
- **Affordable** - $9.99/month vs $29+ competitors
